# Refund & No-Refund Policy

**Last updated: 21 November 2026**

This policy applies to paid digital sessions purchased in Desi Ladki through Google Play.

## 1. What is purchased

The current product configured by the developer is a five-minute, character-specific chat session. The final price, taxes (if applicable), currency and terms shown in Google Play checkout apply to each transaction.

## 2. When a session is activated

A session should be made available only after the backend successfully verifies the Google Play purchase. The five-minute period is measured by the app/backend session timing, not by the time a user merely opens the payment screen.

## 3. Refund requests

Because payment is processed through Google Play, users may request a refund using the Google Play order history/refund process where available. Users may also contact **indianfreelancerguy@gmail.com** with the Google Play order number (never send a purchase token, password, card number or OTP).

We will review reports of:
- a successful charge with no session activation;
- duplicate charges or duplicate order records;
- an app/backend error that prevents the purchased feature from being delivered;
- a purchase that appears to have been cancelled, refunded or revoked incorrectly.

## 4. No automatic cash conversion

A validly delivered digital session is not convertible into cash or transferable to another user. There is no promise of a refund merely because a user changes their mind after using or consuming the session, except where a refund is required by applicable law or Google Play's rules.

## 5. No blanket exclusion of legal rights

Nothing in this policy removes a consumer's statutory rights. Refund eligibility is assessed under applicable law, Google Play policies and the actual transaction circumstances. A blanket “no refunds in all cases” rule is not intended.

## 6. What to include in a support request

Provide the Google Play order number, purchase date/time, product name, app version and a brief description. Do not share passwords, OTPs, full payment-card/bank details or purchase tokens.

## 7. Contact and response

Email **indianfreelancerguy@gmail.com**. Please allow reasonable time for investigation. Applicable statutory complaint acknowledgement and resolution timelines will be followed.
