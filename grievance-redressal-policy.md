# Grievance Redressal Policy

**Last updated: 21 November 2026**

We aim to handle user concerns fairly and transparently.

**Nature of publisher:** Individual developer

The developer has not supplied a postal address, full legal name or a named grievance officer/designation. These details must be added before public launch wherever required by applicable law or platform rules. The email address is a contact email, not a postal address.

## 2. What you may report

You may contact us about privacy, data deletion, payment verification, missing paid sessions, refund concerns, safety, intellectual property, accessibility or other app issues.

## 3. Information to include

Please include:
- a clear description of the complaint;
- the app version and device model, where relevant;
- the Google Play order number for payment complaints;
- the date/time of the issue.

Do not email passwords, OTPs, full card/bank credentials or purchase tokens.

## 4. Acknowledgement and resolution

We will aim to acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within one month, or any shorter/otherwise applicable period required by law. Where an issue requires additional investigation, we will communicate the status where reasonably possible.

## 5. Escalation

If you are dissatisfied with the response, reply to the same email requesting escalation. You may also use applicable consumer or statutory grievance mechanisms available in India.
