## Known product facts used in these drafts

- The app is described by its developer as an entertainment application with scripted, pre-written or predefined chat-style messages.
- The developer says chat messages are stored locally on the user's device.
- The app has an anonymous installation identity and a backend for purchase verification and paid session management.
- The Play product configured by the developer is `chat_5min_49`, described as five minutes of character-specific chat. Regional price display comes from Google Play.
- The developer reports advertising use (AdMob). Production ad identifiers and SDK behaviour must be verified before release.
