# User Safety & Content Policy

**Last updated: 21 November 2026**

Desi Ladki is an 18+ entertainment app using chat-style experiences and predefined or scripted content.

## 1. Age restriction

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and above. Children must not use it. The app operator should ensure age-screening and store age/content declarations match the actual product.

## 2. Fictional and scripted presentation

Characters and conversations are part of an entertainment experience. Messages may be pre-written or scripted and should not be interpreted as proof of live human communication. Profiles must not be used to mislead users about a real person's identity, availability or intentions.

## 3. Safety boundaries

Do not use the app to threaten, harass, exploit, groom, stalk or facilitate harm to anyone. Do not attempt to use it for emergencies, crisis response, medical advice or professional counselling.

## 4. Sensitive information

Avoid sharing your address, identity documents, financial information, passwords, OTPs, intimate images or other sensitive information. Support will never require your password or OTP.

## 5. Reporting

Report misleading content, security issues or safety concerns at **indianfreelancerguy@gmail.com**. If an immediate threat or emergency exists, contact local emergency services; the app is not an emergency response channel.

## 6. Content review

The current product has been described as primarily pre-written content. If user-generated content, free-text prompts, image uploads, voice or live interaction are introduced, this policy and moderation/reporting controls must be updated before release.
