# Recharge & Payment Policy

**Last updated: 21 November 2026**

## 1. Payment provider

In-app digital purchases are offered through Google Play Billing. Google Play presents the price, currency, applicable taxes and payment method before the user confirms the transaction.

## 2. Product

The product configured by the developer is `chat_5min_49`, described as a five-minute chat session with one selected character. The current local price may be displayed in the Google Play purchase interface and can vary by country, tax treatment or price changes. The checkout amount shown by Google Play is the amount to review before confirming.

## 3. Purchase and activation

A payment is not considered activated merely because the user taps the buy button or the Play payment sheet closes. The purchase must be reported and verified with Google Play by the backend. The app should display an active session only after successful verification and session creation.

## 4. Duration

A verified five-minute session is character-specific and expires at the session expiry time returned by the service. Closing and reopening the app does not restart the time. Clearing chat history does not recreate an already used free message or paid session.

## 5. Pending, failed or interrupted payments

A pending transaction does not unlock a paid session. When payment succeeds but the session does not activate, reopen the app and use its purchase-recovery mechanism if available. If it remains unresolved, contact support with the Google Play order number. Do not repeatedly purchase while the first transaction is unresolved.

## 6. Refunds and cancellations

Refunds and cancellations are subject to Google Play processes and applicable consumer law. See the Refund & No-Refund Policy.

## 7. Security

Never share your Google account password, OTP, payment card details or purchase token with support. Support may ask for the Google Play order number for investigation.
