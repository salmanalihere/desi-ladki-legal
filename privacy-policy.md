## 1. Information handled

- **Chat content and local app data:** Chat history, character selection, progress and related chat state are intended to be stored on the user's device. The developer says chat messages are not uploaded to the purchase backend.
- **Installation and session information:** The app/backend may use an anonymous installation identifier and authentication token to associate purchase verification and session status with an installation.
- **Purchase information:** The backend processes purchase-token hashes (not raw purchase tokens in the reviewed database schema), product identifiers, order references, purchase state, character/session identifiers and session timestamps to verify purchases and provide paid sessions.
- **Advertising:** The current Android manifest contains Google's AdMob **test** application ID. The developer has confirmed that no AdMob account has been created and production advertising is not yet configured. This policy must be updated before production ads or any additional analytics/tracking SDKs are enabled. Do not assume the test configuration is equivalent to production.

Google Play handles payment checkout. The app operator does not need users' payment-card or bank credentials.

## 2. Purposes

Information is handled as needed to operate the app, store local chat state, verify purchases, prevent duplicate purchase-token use, activate time-limited sessions, maintain security, respond to support requests and comply with law. Advertising purposes should be added only if/when production advertising is enabled.

## 3. Sharing and service providers

Purchase/session information may be processed by Google Play / Google Payments for billing verification and by the hosting provider operating the purchase-verification backend. If production advertising is later enabled, the policy will identify the advertising provider and relevant data practices before release.

The chat backend is intended to receive purchase/session data rather than chat transcripts. This statement should be checked against production network traffic and all SDK behaviour.

## 4. Storage and security

Chat history is intended to remain on the device. Purchase verification and session records are stored in a backend database. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 5. Retention

Retention means how long information is kept. The backend currently stores anonymous installation records, purchase records and session records. The reviewed backend storage code does not show an automatic deletion schedule for expired sessions or old purchase records. These records may therefore remain until a deletion/cleanup process is implemented or records are removed through an operational process. A specific retention period must not be promised until it is selected and implemented.

Local chat data remains on the device until the user clears app storage, uninstalls the app, or device/backup settings otherwise affect it.

## 6. Your choices and requests

You may clear local app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored chat data from that device, subject to Android backup/device behaviour. To ask about backend purchase/session records associated with an installation, email **indianfreelancerguy@gmail.com**. A deletion request process must be implemented and verified; some transaction records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes.

## 7. Children and age

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and above and is not designed for children. Do not use it if you are under 18.

## 8. Changes

This policy will be updated if advertising, analytics, account features, cloud chat storage or other data processing is introduced.
