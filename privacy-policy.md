# Privacy Policy

**Desi Ladki**  
**Effective / last updated: 21 November 2026**

This policy explains how the Desi Ladki Android application handles information. The app is operated under the name **Xenon** by an individual developer. Privacy contact: **indianfreelancerguy@gmail.com**.

> **Important verification note:** The developer has stated that chat messages are stored locally and that the app uses advertising. The app's source also includes an anonymous installation identity and a backend that processes purchase verification and session records. The exact data collected by advertising SDKs and device diagnostics must be verified against the production SDK configuration before this policy is treated as final.

## 1. The app and its features

Desi Ladki is an entertainment application that provides chat-style experiences using scripted, pre-written or predefined conversational content. It offers optional paid chat sessions through Google Play.

## 2. Information handled

Depending on the app version and use of features, the following information may be processed:

- **Chat content and local app data:** Chat history, character selection, progress and related app state are intended to be stored on the user's device. The developer states that chat messages are not uploaded to the chat backend.
- **Installation and session information:** The app/backend may use an anonymous installation identifier and authentication token to associate purchase verification and session status with an app installation. These are not necessarily a person's real name or email address.
- **Purchase information:** Google Play purchase tokens, product identifiers, order references, purchase state, character/session identifiers and session start/expiry times may be processed by the backend to validate purchases and provide the purchased session. The app should not send payment-card or bank credentials to Xenon; payment processing is handled by Google Play.
- **Advertising and device information:** The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising technology may process device, advertising, app interaction, approximate location, diagnostics or other signals as described in Google's own disclosures and the app's consent settings. The precise categories depend on SDK version, configuration, region and consent state and must be checked before release.

Do not send sensitive personal information in chat fields or support messages.

## 3. Purposes

Information is used as applicable to provide the app, store local chat state, verify purchases, prevent duplicate purchase-token use, provide time-limited sessions, respond to support requests, maintain security, comply with legal duties and display/measure advertising where enabled.

## 4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by:
- **Google Play / Google Payments**, for purchase and payment processing;
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** and related Google services, for advertising and associated operations;
- **Hosting provider**, for the purchase-verification backend and session records;
- competent authorities or professional advisers when disclosure is required or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights.

The app operator does not claim to sell users' chat histories. The chat backend is intended to receive purchase/session data rather than chat transcripts. Confirm this against production network traffic and SDK behaviour.

## 5. Storage and security

Chat history is intended to remain on the device. Purchase verification and session records are stored on the backend. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and Google account secure.

## 6. Retention

Local chat information remains on the device until removed by the user, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled (subject to Android backup/device behaviour). Backend purchase and session records may be retained for security, accounting, dispute handling, legal compliance and fraud prevention for the period reasonably necessary. Exact retention periods and automated deletion schedules must be documented by the operator before publication.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You may clear local app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored chat data from that device, subject to any Android backup. You may contact us to ask about backend purchase/session records associated with an installation. Applicable privacy rights depend on the law in force and your location; requests can be sent to the email below.

## 8. Children and age

The app is intended for adults and includes an 18+ positioning. It is not designed for children. Do not use the app if you are under 18. The operator must ensure age-screening and advertising configurations are consistent with the actual audience and applicable law.

## 9. International processing

Google and hosting providers may process information in locations outside India. Their own terms and privacy documentation may apply. The operator must confirm hosting region and cross-border data flows.
